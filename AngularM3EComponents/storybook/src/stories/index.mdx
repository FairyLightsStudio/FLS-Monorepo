## Components

### Navigation

- **[Navigation Bar](https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-bar/overview)** - Bottom navigation for compact layouts.
- **[Navigation Rail](https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-rail/overview)** - Side navigation rail with Expressive expanded and collapsed states.
- **Navigation Suite** - Responsive scaffold based on [adaptive layout](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/applying-layout/window-size-classes) guidance and related [Jetpack Compose Material source code](https://cs.android.com/androidx/platform/frameworks/support/+/androidx-main:).

### Button

- **Extended FAB Collapsed** - Collapsed state support for Angular Material extended FABs.

## Package Scope

These packages are designed to work with Angular Material and consume Angular Material system tokens such as `--mat-sys-*`.

They are third-party implementations by FairyLights Studio, not official Angular or Google components.
